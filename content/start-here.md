Welcome to my personal realm! I hope you enjoy your visit!

I write here about my ideas, projects, readings, and more.

I know that my ideas and views will change with time and that I will disagree with or cringe at the things I put out long ago. My hope is that I continue to grow and that past posts are seen as a step in that growth.

I am starting this site, October 2026, because I am hoping to create projects and ideas and share them with others. And I'm welling up with creative ideas and no place to put them. However, inspired by all the bloggers out there, I am ready to put myself out there and hopefully this time not delete it!

Another big reason is that I anticipate a localized yet profound renaissance in personal websites. As I write this, there is no digital outlet with as much creative control as having a personal website. However, where bloggers once had to learn HTML to build their funky pages, then forced to click through WordPress themes to choose their own template site, the incoming wave of AI tools and open information sharing will allow users to create their own digital oasis as easily as they can design their dream home or order groceries.
